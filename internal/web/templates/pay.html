New payment

Move money between accounts. The payment posts as one balanced transaction — or not at all. Try sending more than the balance: the ledger will refuse, and nothing moves.

Dollars and cents, e.g. 25 or 25.50. Both accounts must share a currency — no FX in v1.

This form carries an idempotency key — submitting it twice can never pay twice.
